Frequently asked questions

Everything you're wondering, answered.

What does a Hire a Wifey service include?+
More than traditional cleaning — depending on your booking, your Wifey can assist with cleaning, laundry, ironing, bed changes, dishes, home organisation, tidying and basic meal preparation.
Are you insured and police-checked?+
Yes. Hire a Wifey is fully insured and our Wifeys hold current police checks — trust and professionalism in your home matter to us.
Do I have to book cleaning?+
No. You can book laundry, ironing, bed changes or organisation on their own, or combine them with cleaning in the same visit.
Can I give my Wifey a list of jobs?+
Yes. Tell us your priorities and your Wifey works through them during the booked time.
Do you offer regular cleaning?+
Yes — weekly and fortnightly services are available, subject to current availability.
Do you provide cleaning products?+
Where applicable, our Wifeys can bring the equipment and products needed. Let us know if you'd prefer we use yours.
Do you accept NDIS and DVA clients?+
Yes — we welcome eligible self-managed and plan-managed NDIS participants, and eligible DVA clients requiring approved household assistance.
What areas do you service?+
Hervey Bay and surrounding suburbs including Pialba, Scarness, Torquay, Urangan, Point Vernon, Kawungan, Urraween and more — see our service area above.
How do I book?+
Click any "Book a Wifey" button on this page — it takes you straight to our booking page.
